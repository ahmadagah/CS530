## 1. Dated timeline of Square launches, plan/pricing overhauls and major features (2023 to Oct 2026)

### Takeaway
Since May 2025 Square ships in twice-yearly "Square Releases" events (May 2025, Oct 2025), plus monthly "What's New From Square" community roundups. The biggest changes for a service business are the Oct 2025 move from 18 à la carte subscriptions to Free/Plus/Premium plans, the unified Square POS app with modes (May 2025), and a fast run of AI features: Square AI (2025), Square Assistant SMS for appointments, Managerbot agent (Apr 2026), ChatGPT/Claude integrations (Jul 2026) and an AI voice kiosk (Oct 2026). Most of the 2026 AI and hardware work targets restaurants and retail rather than mobile services.

### Inferences
- The AI arc runs: generative content tools (2023) → Square AI Q&A (2025) → Square Assistant SMS for appointments (2025) → Managerbot agent (Apr 2026) → external AI-assistant discovery (ChatGPT/Claude/Alexa+, Jul 2026) → voice kiosk (Oct 2026). Almost all of the agentic features target food and beverage or retail, not mobile services, which leaves room for a detailing-specific dispatch app.
- The Oct 2025 plan overhaul matters for the replacement case. A detailer on Square Free now pays 3.3% + 30¢ online (since Jan 2026) and needs Plus ($49/mo) for features such as appointment buffer time. That cost baseline is what the custom app must beat or match.
- The Reader SDK retirement (Apr 2026) means any custom app that takes in-person card payments through Square hardware must use the Mobile Payments SDK.

## 3. What Square offers mobile, field, home and automotive service businesses (and what it doesn't)

### Takeaway
Square markets "Home & Repair," "Contractors" and "Automotive/Auto Shop" pages that bundle Square Appointments (booking at the customer's location), Invoices (estimates → invoices, deposits, payment schedules, projects) and the POS "services mode." It names car wash and detailing as target businesses. It has no real field-dispatch layer: no travel-time calculation, service-area limits, routing, GPS tracking, technician job-status workflow, vehicle records or per-stage inspection photos. Mobile detailers rely on manual workarounds or third-party apps.

### Cited Findings
**Square's service-vertical positioning**
- Square has US pages for **Home and Repair Service Software**, **Contractor Software** and **Auto Shop Software**. The contractor page claims sellers can "create estimates, schedule workers, track jobs in real time, and send invoices, all while accepting payments on-site or online." — [Square: Home & Repair](https://squareup.com/us/en/services/home-and-repair); [Square: Contractors](https://squareup.com/us/en/services/contractors); [Square: Automotive](https://squareup.com/us/en/services/automotive)
- Square lists the service sellers it targets as "contractors, electricians, HVAC businesses, cleaners, home repair professionals, consultants, event providers, automotive businesses, and other service sellers." — [Square: Estimates](https://squareup.com/us/en/invoices/features/estimates)
- Square's Canada and Australia "Automotive & Mechanic Scheduling Software" pages position Square Appointments for **car wash and detailing**, with online booking, payments and client reminders, and integrations with **Xero and QuickBooks**. — [Square AU: Automotive](https://squareup.com/au/en/solutions/automotive); [Square CA: Automotive](https://squareup.com/ca/en/solutions/automotive)

**Estimates → invoices → projects workflow**
- Estimates are itemized, customer approvals are tracked, and accepted work turns into invoices without re-entry. **Eligible paid plans convert accepted estimates to invoices automatically.** — [Square: Estimates](https://squareup.com/us/en/invoices/features/estimates)

## 4. App Marketplace integrations relevant to mobile detailing and field service

### Takeaway
Field-service and detailing platforms sit on top of Square mainly as a **payment processor** (card readers and payment sync back to the platform's invoices) or through **Zapier/Integrately** triggers. Jobber and Urable have direct Square payment integrations. Housecall Pro connects only through Zapier-style connectors. AI receptionist vendors plug into Square Appointments. No evidence was found of Mobile Tech RX integrating with Square.

### Cited Findings
- **Jobber + Square:** payments accepted through Square sync back into Jobber, keeping invoices matched. Clients can pay online from Jobber invoices, and cards can be taken at the job site. — [Jobber: Square integration](https://www.getjobber.com/integrations/square/)
- Jobber is noted for broad integrations, including Stripe and Square for payments. — [FieldPulse comparison 2026](https://www.fieldpulse.com/resources/blog/housecall-pro-vs-jobber)
- Zapier and Integrately offer Jobber ↔ Square automations (e.g., new clients or payments). The Zapier page is old. — [Zapier: Jobber + Square](https://zapier.com/apps/jobber/integrations/square); [Integrately](https://integrately.com/integrations/jobber/square)
- **Housecall Pro + Square:** only third-party connectors were found (Zapier triggers that move new customers or payments into Square). No first-party Housecall Pro–Square sync was found; Housecall Pro has its own payments (Instapay). — [Zapier: Housecall Pro + Square](https://zapier.com/apps/housecall-pro/integrations/square); [FieldPulse](https://www.fieldpulse.com/resources/blog/housecall-pro-vs-jobber)
- **Urable** (detailing, PPF and wrap CRM) "integrates with Stripe and Square but you can use any processors of your choosing." Its card readers are compatible with readers from UrPay, Stripe and Square. — [Urable: Payment Processing](https://urable.com/payment-processing/)
- Urable's Express plan (from $70/mo or $840/yr) reportedly includes the Stripe and Square payment integrations. — [QuoteIQ: Top CRMs for mobile detailing](https://myquoteiq.com/top-10-crms-for-mobile-detailing-businesses-in-2026/); [QuoteIQ vs Urable](https://myquoteiq.com/compare/urable/) (competitor content)
- **Mobile Tech RX:** its detailing page markets payment processing and scheduling and lists Square and Urable under "Reasons to Switch," positioning itself as a replacement rather than an integration. — [Mobile Tech RX: Detailing](https://www.mobiletechrx.com/detailing/)
- **AI receptionists on Square:** Square's App Marketplace has an "AI Receptionist" listing. Third-party vendors (NextPhone, Voka AI) advertise Square Appointments integrations that answer calls and book into Square. Hostie publishes a guide to connecting AI phone ordering to Square POS. — [Square App Marketplace: AI Receptionist](https://squareup.com/us/en/app-marketplace/app/ai-receptionist); [NextPhone](https://www.getnextphone.com/integrations/square-appointments); [Voka AI](https://vokaai.com/compare/best-ai-receptionist-for-square-appointments); [Hostie](https://hostie.ai/resources/ai-phone-ordering-square-pos-integration-guide)
- **Accounting:** Square's automotive pages promote **Xero and QuickBooks** integrations. — [Square CA: Automotive](https://squareup.com/ca/en/solutions/automotive)
- For comparison, among field-service apps Jobber's QuickBooks Online sync is one-way (invoices and payments pushed to QBO), while Housecall Pro's is real-time two-way. — [Steph's Books](https://stephsbooks.com/blog/hvac-software-quickbooks-integration)
- **Third-party/developer channel:** Square's ChatGPT and Claude integrations (Jul 2026) show Square now exposes catalog and ordering to outside AI agents, but only for food and beverage online ordering so far. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260701594157/en/Square-Introduces-New-ChatGPT-and-Claude-Integrations-Helping-Sellers-Reach-Customers-Through-AI-Powered-Discovery)

### Inferences
- There are three integration patterns other apps use with Square:
  1. **Payments-only**: the app owns jobs, invoices and customers, and Square is just the processor and card reader (Jobber, Urable).
  2. **Trigger sync via Zapier**: customers and payments are copied between systems (Housecall Pro).
  3. **Booking-layer plug-ins**: the app reads and writes Square Appointments, catalog and customers (AI receptionists).
- A Sheen dispatch app that "replaces Square entirely" would most likely drop Square and use a direct processor (as Urable allows). Alternatively it could keep Square only as a payments rail during migration, with a one-time import of Square customers, catalog and bookings through Square's APIs (covered by the developer-API researcher).
- Detailing-specific platforms (Urable, Mobile Tech RX, DetailDeck, CalMov, Detaild, QuoteIQ) all market against Square's lack of service-area, vehicle and photo features. That confirms these are the features a detailer's custom app should prioritize.

### Gaps
- The live Square App Marketplace listings (and whether Jobber, Urable, Housecall Pro, ServiceM8, Setmore and Acuity are listed there) could not be read because squareup.com was blocked.
- **ServiceM8, Setmore, Acuity Scheduling** and Square's native **Zapier** app were not researched individually in this session (tool-call budget).
- What data each integration syncs (customers? catalog? appointments? payouts?) is documented only at a high level for Jobber. Urable's Square sync depth is unknown.
- No source confirmed whether Mobile Tech RX accepts Square as a processor.

Square's newest products (Bitcoin Payments, Neighborhoods, the unified Free/Plus/Premium plans, Managerbot, ChatGPT/Claude integrations, Payroll) are US-first or US-only. Card acceptance spans 8 countries, but hardware and accounts are locked to the country of purchase. Pricing and rates differ by country.
